Background

From the building site to code

Dione started working at the age of 10, as a bricklayer. By 15 he was already drawing house plans and running building projects from start to finish.

In 2005 he moved to the United States. After getting through the adjustment and the language barrier, he opened his own high-end flooring and finishing company and stayed in the sector until 2012.

When he realised that growing at scale meant trading physical effort for strategy, he moved into digital entrepreneurship and relationship marketing. According to Dione himself, in 2013 a fresh start with US$ 360 turned into more than US$ 850,000 in earnings within a few months.

In the financial market since 2012, from 2015 onwards, after travelling through European countries studying finance, he turned his attention to cryptocurrencies and trading, where he established himself as an investor, mentor and strategist.

From 2013 to this day he has been leading people and teams. Once he entered cryptocurrencies, he also began teaching how to store digital assets safely. According to him, his network now brings together more than 120,000 people around the world.

At the head of Zebubit, a company with more than 38 years of history, he brings that experience together into practical solutions for companies and individuals.
